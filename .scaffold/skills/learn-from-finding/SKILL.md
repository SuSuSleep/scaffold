# Learn From Finding

## Goal

Convert a discovered problem into durable reusable knowledge when appropriate.

## Entry Conditions

- A relevant finding or observed problem exists.

## Phases

### Phase — Establish Finding Context

#### Goal

Establish the evidence, conditions, and project context surrounding the finding.

#### Required Outcome

The finding, its evidence, relevant conditions, and applicable project knowledge are understood.

#### Suggested Skills

- collect-context

### Phase — Determine Root Cause and Scope

#### Goal

Identify the cause of the finding and whether it can be safely generalized.

#### Required Outcome

Root cause, affected scope, and safe generalization are identified from the finding's evidence and conditions. Distinguish observed symptoms from supported causal explanations, test those explanations against counterexamples or alternative causes where evidence permits, and retain uncertainty where generalization is not justified.

### Phase — Reconcile Reusable Knowledge

#### Goal

Preserve justified learning as durable knowledge when appropriate.

#### Required Outcome

The proposed durable knowledge is defined with its evidence, justified scope, classification decisions, and material relationships, then supplied to `reconcile-project-change`. This Workflow does not directly accept the resulting Project Knowledge.

### Phase — Link Resolution and Evidence

#### Goal

Connect the finding's resolution to evidence that it has been addressed.

#### Required Outcome

The solution change and verification are linked where appropriate.

#### Suggested Skills

- verify-change

## Required Outcomes

- The finding is converted into durable reusable knowledge when appropriate.
