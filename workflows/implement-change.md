# Implement Change

## Goal

Bring the implementation into compliance with an accepted change while preserving knowledge consistency.

## Entry Conditions

- The requested change and relevant constraints are understood.

## Required Outcomes

- Sufficient context and affected requirements are identified.
- Required durable knowledge is updated.
- Implementation and proportionate verification are complete.
- Durable discoveries are reconciled.

## Suggested Skills

- collect-context
- analyze-impact
- implement-with-tdd
- verify-change
